# Code of conduct

AlgoVerse is a place to learn, and people learn best when they feel safe to ask "obvious"
questions and to be wrong in public.

**Expected**

- Be welcoming to beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
- Critique designs and code, not people. Explain the why, with numbers where you can.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give credit: link to where an idea came from.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or posting other people's copyrighted material.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the
comment or contact the maintainers through the repository.
